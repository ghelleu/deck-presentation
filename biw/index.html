Annex A14

From Feed to Boutique

Our competitors compete on price, discounts or scale. Bixler's competes on heritage, artistry and experience.

Heirloom storytelling

Instagram Reels and TikTok showing heirloom transformations from our atelier: the inherited ring, the redesign sketch, the finished Heritage piece. A craft our competitors don't lead with.

Geo-targeted campaigns

Paid social campaigns around our location for our core 25 to 44 audience: engaged couples and clients who have inherited family jewelry. Heritage and craftsmanship, not price.

From feed to boutique

Every post leads to our digital concierge: a virtual heirloom consultation by Instagram message or on the website, then a private appointment in store.

The new website

A Shopify site built as a digital extension of the boutique, to Rolex's visual and editorial standards, with a digital concierge for guidance, appointments and tailored recommendations.

The goal is simple: to inspire discovery online and create the desire to experience Bixler's in person.
